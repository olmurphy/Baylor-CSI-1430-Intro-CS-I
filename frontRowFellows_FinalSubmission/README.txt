//Movement and Controls

LeftArrow  - Move ship left
RightArrow - Move ship right
SPACE      - Shoot
F          - Pause
S          - To Start / Restart
C          - To Advance

//Lore

Fed up with the endless amounts of homework, a rogue group of students decides to challenge 
Dr.Booth's authority. Being a benevolent god he agrees to allow the group to pass the class 
without doing the homework if and only if they can defeat him and his army of intro 1 and 
intro to algorithms students. You have been choosen as the sole pilot of the one ship Booth 
has given you to challenge him. Your fellow group members provide support through the 
communications systems. Your group's grade depends on your performance. Do not fail them.

Enemy Type:
Level 1: C-Level intro 1 students
Level 2: B-Level intro 1 students
Level 3: A-Level intro 1 students
Level 4: B-Level intro to Alogirithms students
Level 5: A-Level intro to Algorithims students
Boss Level: Lab TA's
